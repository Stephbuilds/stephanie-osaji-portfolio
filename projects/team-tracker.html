Team Tracker

A lightweight daily status tracker: each team member logs what they're working on and its status, giving the whole team visibility without standup meetings or a heavyweight project management tool.

Node.js Express SQLite Vanilla JS

The problem

Small teams often don't need a full project management suite. They need a fast way to answer "what is everyone working on right now, and how far along is it?" without the overhead of logins, tickets, or configuration.

Key decisions

Deliberately low-friction identity

No passwords, no accounts. Each person picks their name from a roster, and that identity is remembered locally. It's an explicit tradeoff: this model is designed for a small, trusted internal team where the goal is visibility, not access control.

Status as a first-class, editable field

Every logged activity carries a status (Not Started / In Progress / Completed) that only its owner can change, with the time since the last status change shown inline, so staleness is visible at a glance, not something you have to ask about.
